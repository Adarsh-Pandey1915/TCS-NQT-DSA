TCS NQT & Data Analyst Roadmap

16-Week Plan to Clear the TCS Integrated Assessment (Ninja/Digital/Prime) & Build Industry Data Skills

⏱️ Hour 1: TCS Aptitude

Numerical, Verbal & Reasoning Math

⏱️ Hour 2: Data Skills

SQL, Excel & Power BI

⏱️ Hour 3: DSA / Coding

Arrays, Strings & Advanced Logic

Phase 1: TCS Foundation & Core Data Basics (Weeks 1 - 4)

Week 1-2: TCS Aptitude (Part A) & Python Basics

TCS NQT DSA
TCS Foundation Section Target (75 Mins): Focus strictly on Percentages, Profit & Loss, Ages, Time & Work (Numerical); Syllogisms & Seating Arrangements (Reasoning); Sentence Completion & Error Spotting (Verbal).
  • Practice 20 NQT Aptitude questions daily.
  • Learn Python basics for Data: loops, lists, dictionaries.
  • Solve 15 Easy Array/String problems for the NQT Coding Section.
🤖 Where AI Can Help: Paste confusing Syllogisms or "Pipes & Cisterns" math questions into ChatGPT and prompt: "Explain the underlying mathematical formula for this problem step-by-step for a beginner, without just giving the final answer."
TCS NQT Syllabus & Patterns

Week 3-4: Relational Databases & Core SQL

SQL TCS NQT
Where to Emphasize: For technical interviews, mastering multi-table JOINs, GROUP BY, and handling NULL values is the absolute core of the data analyst role.
  • SQL: Understand Primary/Foreign Keys, INNER/LEFT JOINs, and WHERE vs. HAVING.
  • NQT Advanced Math (Part B): Shift aptitude practice to Probability, Permutations & Combinations, and Data Interpretation.
Interactive SQL Tutorial GFG NQT Prep
Phase 2: Advanced NQT Coding & Data Wrangling (Weeks 5 - 8)

Week 5-6: Advanced SQL & TCS NQT Coding (Part B)

SQL DSA
TCS Advanced Coding Target (90 Mins): You must solve 2 coding problems to unlock the Digital (7 LPA) and Prime (9-11 LPA) packages. Focus on Sorting, Searching algorithms, and String manipulation.
  • SQL: Master Window Functions (ROW_NUMBER, RANK, LEAD, LAG) and CTEs.
  • DSA: Practice standard array manipulation and basic recursion problems.
Top 50 SQL Study Plan

Week 7-8: Modern Excel & NQT Full-Length Mocks

Excel TCS NQT
Where to Emphasize: Stop at basic Excel formulas (XLOOKUP, SUMIFS). Focus heavily on Power Query to automate data cleaning.
  • Learn to use Power Query to append, merge, and unpivot messy CSV datasets.
  • Crucial: Take 2 full-length 190-minute TCS NQT Mock tests. Remember, there is NO negative marking, but you cannot switch between sections freely!
Chandoo Excel Automation
Phase 3: Business Intelligence & AI Strategy (Weeks 9 - 12)

Week 9-10: Power BI, Data Modeling & DAX

Power BI
Where to Emphasize: Do not just drag and drop charts. Master the Star Schema. If your data model is bad, your dashboard will fail.
  • Understand Filter Context vs. Row Context in DAX.
  • Learn core DAX measures: CALCULATE, SUMX, FILTER, TOTALYTD.
🤖 Where AI Can Help: DAX is confusing. Prompt AI: "I have a Fact table of Sales and a Dimension table of Dates. Write a DAX measure using CALCULATE to find a 30-day rolling average, and explain the filter context."
Free MS Learn PL-300 Path

Week 11-12: Business Strategy & Critical Thinking

Strategy
  • Learn business metrics: Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Lifetime Value (LTV), and Churn Rate.
  • Hypothesis Testing: Learn how to frame an analytical question (e.g., "Did the new UI drop cart checkout rates?").
StatQuest (Hypothesis Testing)
Phase 4: TCS Interview Prep & Portfolio (Weeks 13 - 16)

Week 13-14: TCS Technical/HR Rounds & Storytelling

Interviews Storytelling
TCS Interview Reality: For Digital/Prime roles, the technical interview involves deep questions on your final year project, DBMS (SQL), and sometimes live coding. HR will test communication and relocation flexibility.
  • Practice explaining your technical projects without using jargon (Executive Storytelling).
  • Prepare clear answers for standard Managerial and HR round questions.
🤖 Where AI Can Help: Give an AI your resume and prompt: "Act as a TCS Technical Interviewer for a Digital role. Ask me 3 challenging questions based on my SQL and Power BI projects, and grade my responses."

Week 15-16: Portfolio Building & Final Review

Portfolio Projects
Where to Emphasize: Build two end-to-end projects. Do NOT use generic datasets like Titanic or Iris. Host your code on GitHub.
  • Build Project 1: Revenue / E-Commerce Operations Dashboard.
  • Build Project 2: Customer Retention / Churn SQL Analysis.
  • Format a 1-page ATS-friendly resume highlighting "TCS NQT Score" (once received) and portfolio links.
